A Free 10,000 Shots App Built Around Your Team

Most shot trackers are solo counters. This one turns the challenge into a team competition.

The 10,000 shot challenge is a great habit — shoot pucks off-ice all summer and log them. The popular apps and paper trackers do the counting well, but they're built for a kid practicing alone: you shoot, you type a number, you save. It can feel like homework.

Hockey Shot Challenge is a free alternative built around the part that actually keeps kids going back to the driveway: competition with their teammates.

Why the team angle matters

  • Kids show up for the leaderboard. Seeing your name climb past a teammate's is a stronger pull than a private number.
  • Squad battles add a weekly rivalry. Every Monday you're matched against another player; most shots by Sunday wins.
  • Coaches get visibility. A dashboard shows who logged shots this week — so off-ice work is actually seen, not invisible.
  • It spreads through the team. One coach sets it up, and the whole roster is competing in the group chat within days.
